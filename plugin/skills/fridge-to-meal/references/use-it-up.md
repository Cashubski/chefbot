# Use it up: leftovers and odd amounts

Keep times assume a fridge below 5 °C. "Freezes" means portion it first.

## Dairy and eggs

- **Egg whites** (keeps 4 days; freezes 12 months, one per ice-cube slot): meringues or pavlova; a coconut macaroon; an egg-white omelette with herbs; clarify a stock; brush pastry for shine.
- **Egg yolks** (2 days under a film of water; freeze whisked with a pinch of salt or sugar): custard or crème anglaise; mayonnaise or aioli; carbonara; enrich a risotto or mash; a yolk in bread dough for colour.
- **Sour or on-the-turn milk** (use the same day if it only smells slightly sour; throw away if curdled and off): pancakes, scones, soda bread or a cake, where the acidity helps the rise; a quick ricotta if it is just turning.
- **Half a tub of cream** (3 days): a pan sauce, scrambled eggs, mashed potato, a gratin; whip and freeze in dollops for coffee or desserts.
- **Yoghurt near its date** (a week past the date is usually fine if it smells clean): marinade for chicken; tzatziki or raita; overnight oats; flatbread dough with flour and a pinch of baking powder; thin as a salad dressing.
- **Cheese ends and rinds** (hard cheese a month wrapped in paper; parmesan rinds freeze indefinitely): rinds into a soup, stew or risotto for savoury depth, lifted out before serving; ends grated into mac and cheese, a cheese sauce, an omelette, or baked into crackers.
- **Butter wrappers**: grease a cake tin.

## Bread and grains

- **Stale bread** (2 to 3 days at room temperature, longer as crumbs or croutons; freezes 3 months): panzanella or fattoush; French toast; bread and butter pudding; breadcrumbs for coating and topping; croutons in oil and garlic; thickening for gazpacho or a Tuscan bean soup (ribollita).
- **Cooked rice** (1 day; cool within an hour; reheat once until steaming): egg fried rice; kimchi fried rice; congee; arancini; rice pudding; stuffed peppers.
- **Cooked pasta** (3 days): a frittata; a pasta bake; fried in butter and cheese until crisp at the edges; cold in a salad with a sharp dressing.
- **Cooked potatoes** (3 days): bubble and squeak; potato cakes with an egg; a tortilla; smashed and roasted; a potato salad.
- **Open packet of tortillas or pitta** (a week; freezes): quesadillas; tortilla chips (cut, oil, 8 minutes at 200 °C); fattoush; a wrap for leftovers.

## Vegetables and herbs

- **Wilting herbs** (a week in a glass of water, loosely bagged; freeze chopped in oil in an ice-cube tray): a herb sauce (salsa verde, chimichurri, pesto with any nut or seed); stir through rice or couscous; a herb butter; a green salad dressing; parsley and coriander stems go into stocks and dals.
- **Half a lemon or lime** (a week cut side down on a plate): squeeze over anything fried or roasted; a vinaigrette; zest into sugar or salt for later; a quick pickled onion; lemon water.
- **Half an onion** (a week, wrapped): base of nearly everything; pickled in vinegar and sugar for 20 minutes as a topping.
- **Celery leaves and outer stalks** (a week): soups, stocks, a stir-fry; the leaves as a herb in salads.
- **Carrot tops, beetroot leaves, radish tops** (2 days): a pesto, sautéed like spinach, or into a soup.
- **Vegetable peelings and trimmings** (freeze in a bag until there is enough): a stock, simmered 45 minutes with a bay leaf; potato peelings roasted in oil and salt as crisps.
- **Tired salad leaves** (use the same day): wilt into a soup, a dal or a pasta sauce; blend into a green smoothie; a lettuce soup with potato and stock.
- **Limp carrots, celery and peppers**: revive in iced water for an hour, or roast; a soup; a slaw with a sharp dressing.
- **Half a cabbage** (2 weeks wrapped): coleslaw; okonomiyaki; a quick kimchi-style pickle; fried with bacon or caraway; bubble and squeak; minestrone.
- **Soft tomatoes** (use within a day or two): a sauce, roasted with garlic and thyme, gazpacho, shakshuka, a tomato jam.
- **Soft mushrooms** (2 days): duxelles cooked down with butter and shallot, which freezes; a soup; mushroom stroganoff.
- **Half a tin of sweetcorn, beans or chickpeas** (3 days in a covered non-metal container): into a salad, a soup, a fritter, hummus; chickpeas roasted with spices.
- **Half a tin of coconut milk** (3 days; freezes 2 months): a dal, a curry base, rice and peas, a smoothie, overnight oats, a coconut pancake batter.
- **Half a tin of tomatoes or passata** (3 days; freezes): shakshuka for one, a pizza base sauce, a quick soup with stock and basil, chilli or bolognese topped up.
- **Chickpea water (aquafaba)** (4 days; freezes): whip as an egg-white substitute for meringue or mousse (3 tbsp per egg white); a vegan mayonnaise; froth for cocktails.

## Fruit

- **Overripe bananas** (freeze peeled, whole or sliced): banana bread; pancakes; a smoothie; "nice cream" blended from frozen; porridge sweetener.
- **Soft apples and pears** (a week): a crumble, a compote, baked with cinnamon, grated into porridge or a slaw.
- **Berries going soft** (freeze on a tray): a compote for yoghurt, a smoothie, a quick jam, folded into muffin batter.
- **Citrus about to turn**: juice and freeze in ice-cube trays; zest first; a marmalade or a lemon curd.

## Meat, fish and cooked dishes

- **Roast meat** (2 days, UK FSA; 3 to 4 days, USDA; freezes 3 months): a pie with gravy and vegetables; tacos or quesadillas; fried rice; a sandwich with pickles; a stock from the bones and carcass, simmered 3 hours.
- **Roast chicken carcass**: stock or a chicken noodle soup; freeze the carcass until there is time.
- **Half a packet of bacon, chorizo or ham** (3 days opened; freezes): carbonara, a quiche, a bean stew, fried rice, a soup base, a toastie.
- **A single sausage or two** (2 days; freezes): a sausage pasta with fennel seed, a bean casserole, crumbled into a stuffing.
- **Cooked fish** (1 day): fishcakes; kedgeree; a fish pie; flaked into a salad or a wrap.
- **Leftover curry, chilli, stew** (2 to 3 days; freezes 3 months): a jacket potato topping; a pie under pastry; with eggs baked in; thinned into a soup; in a toastie.
- **Gravy and stock** (2 days; freezes): a base for a soup or a risotto; reheated to a rolling boil before use.

## Drinks and sweet things

- **Open wine** (3 to 5 days with the cork in, in the fridge; freezes in ice-cube trays): deglaze a pan; a risotto; a stew; poach pears in red; a white wine butter sauce. Never cook with wine that tastes vinegary unless it is going into a vinaigrette.
- **Flat beer**: a batter for fish or onion rings; a bread; a stew (Carbonnade, Irish stew with stout).
- **Cold coffee**: an iced latte; a tiramisu; a coffee syrup for cake; a rub for beef with brown sugar and smoked paprika.
- **Jam and marmalade ends** (months in the fridge): a glaze for ham or carrots; a vinaigrette; bread and butter pudding; thinned as a sauce for pancakes.
- **Chocolate ends, broken biscuits**: a rocky road; a crumble topping; a base for a cheesecake.

## When not to use it up

Rice or cooked food left out overnight, meat or fish that smells sour or is slimy, bulging or leaking tins, mould on soft food, and anything past its "use by" date. Those go in the bin; see the food-safety skill.
