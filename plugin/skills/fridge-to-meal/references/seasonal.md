# Seasonal produce

Use this when suggesting what to buy, what to swap in, or why something tastes better or costs less. Say "in season now" only after checking the user's hemisphere and month; if unknown, ask or give both. Imports blur the lines, so present these as "at its best and cheapest", not as rules.

## How to use it

- Swap an out-of-season ingredient for the in-season equivalent that does the same job: courgette for asparagus in spring pasta, leeks for spring onions in winter, frozen peas any time.
- In season usually means cheaper and better, and local produce travels less; say so in a line when it changes the choice.
- Frozen peas, spinach, berries and sweetcorn are picked and frozen at their peak; they are a fair swap in any month.
