# Substitution reference

Ratios are by weight or volume as stated. "Effect" is what the eater notices.

## Fats

| Missing | Use | Ratio | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Butter (baking) | Oil | 80 ml oil per 100 g butter | moister, less flavour, softer crust; not for laminated or creamed recipes |
| Butter (baking) | Margarine or vegan block | 1:1 | similar; check it is 75–80 % fat |
| Butter (cooking) | Olive oil, ghee | 1:1 | ghee for high heat; olive oil flavour shows |
| Oil (baking) | Melted butter | 1:1 by volume | richer, slightly drier |
| Oil (baking) | Apple purée or yoghurt | replace up to half | lighter, denser, less keeping time |
| Lard or shortening | Butter | 1:1 | pastry less flaky, more flavour |
| Cream (sauces) | Crème fraîche, full-fat Greek yoghurt | 1:1, add off the heat | yoghurt can split if boiled; add a teaspoon of cornflour |
| Cream (whipping) | Coconut cream (chilled, solid part) | 1:1 | coconut flavour; whips less stiff |
| Coconut milk | Cream + water or oat milk + 1 tbsp oil | 1:1 | lighter, no coconut note |

## Dairy and eggs

| Missing | Use | Ratio | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Milk | Water + 1 tbsp butter per 250 ml, or plant milk | 1:1 | less rich; oat milk closest in sauces |
| Buttermilk | Milk + 1 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar per 250 ml, rest 5 min | 1:1 | identical in baking |
| Buttermilk | Yoghurt thinned with milk | 1:1 | identical in baking |
| Sour cream | Greek yoghurt | 1:1 | tangier, add off the heat |
| Cream cheese | Mascarpone, thick strained yoghurt | 1:1 | sweeter / tangier |
| Parmesan | Pecorino, Grana Padano; nutritional yeast (vegan) | 1:1; 2 tbsp per 25 g | pecorino saltier; yeast gives umami, no melt |
| Egg (binding) | 1 tbsp ground flax + 3 tbsp water, rest 10 min | per egg | slightly denser, fine in muffins and burgers |
| Egg (whipping) | 3 tbsp aquafaba | per egg; 2 tbsp per white | whips for meringue and mousse, less stable: add ¼ tsp cream of tartar |
| Egg (moisture, cakes) | 60 g mashed banana or apple purée | per egg | denser, flavour of the fruit |
| Egg wash | Milk, cream, or oil; plant milk + maple syrup | brush | less shine than egg |

## Flours, starches and leavening

| Missing | Use | Ratio | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Self-raising flour | Plain flour + 2 tsp baking powder per 150 g | per 150 g | identical |
| Plain flour (thickening) | Cornflour | half the amount, slaked in cold water | glossier, clearer; do not boil long |
| Cornflour | Arrowroot, potato starch | 1:1 | similar; arrowroot does not reheat well |
| Bread flour | Plain flour | 1:1 | less chew, slightly flatter loaf |
| Cake flour | Plain flour minus 2 tbsp per 125 g, plus 2 tbsp cornflour | per 125 g | close |
| Baking powder | ¼ tsp bicarbonate of soda + ½ tsp cream of tartar | per 1 tsp | bake immediately |
| Bicarbonate of soda | Baking powder | 3× the amount | milder rise; a little saltier; reduce acid in recipe |
| Breadcrumbs | Crushed crackers, oats, cornflakes, panko | 1:1 | panko crisper; oats softer |
| Yeast (fresh) | Instant dried yeast | ⅓ the weight (15 g fresh ≈ 5 g instant) | identical |

## Sugars and sweeteners

| Missing | Use | Ratio | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Caster sugar | Granulated, blitzed 10 s | 1:1 | identical |
| Brown sugar | Caster + 1 tbsp molasses or treacle per 200 g | per 200 g | identical |
| Sugar | Honey or maple syrup | 170 g per 200 g sugar, reduce liquid by 3 tbsp, oven 15 °C lower | browns faster, moister |
| Icing sugar | Blitz granulated to powder + 1 tsp cornflour per 100 g | per 100 g | slightly grainier |
| Golden syrup | Honey, light corn syrup, maple syrup | 1:1 | flavour differs; honey browns faster |

## Acids, alcohol and umami

| Missing | Use | Ratio | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lemon juice | Lime juice, white wine vinegar | 1:1; vinegar ½ | lime floral; vinegar sharper |
| White wine (cooking) | Stock + 1 tsp vinegar or lemon per 100 ml; dry vermouth | 1:1 | vermouth closest |
| Red wine (cooking) | Stock + 1 tsp balsamic or red wine vinegar per 100 ml; grape or pomegranate juice (reduce sugar) | 1:1 | less depth; longer reduction helps |
| Soy sauce | Tamari (gluten-free), coconut aminos (use 1½×), Worcestershire (contains fish) + salt | 1:1 | aminos sweeter |
| Fish sauce | Light soy + a squeeze of lime + pinch of sugar; 1 anchovy per tbsp | 1:1 | less funk |
| Worcestershire sauce | Soy + splash of vinegar + pinch of sugar | 1:1 | close enough in stews |
| Stock cube | Miso (1 tsp per 250 ml), Marmite (½ tsp), parmesan rind in the pot | per 250 ml | miso saltier; add gradually |
| Tomato purée | Ketchup (reduce sugar), reduced passata | 1:1; 3:1 | ketchup sweeter and acidic |

## Herbs, spices and aromatics

| Missing | Use | Ratio | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fresh herb | Dried herb | ⅓ the amount, add early | woody herbs (thyme, oregano, rosemary) dry well; basil, coriander, parsley do not: use another fresh herb |
| Garlic clove | ¼ tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp garlic paste | per clove | powder flatter; paste saltier |
| Fresh ginger | ¼ tsp ground ginger per 1 cm | per cm | ground is warmer, less bright |
| Shallot | Onion (less) + a pinch of sugar | ½ onion per 2 shallots | coarser |
| Spring onion | Chives, leek (white, finely sliced) | 1:1 | chives raw only |
| Chilli (fresh) | ¼ tsp chilli flakes, ⅛ tsp cayenne | per chilli | flakes more bitter, cayenne hotter |
| Garam masala | ½ tsp each cumin, coriander, ¼ tsp cinnamon, pinch of clove and cardamom | per 1½ tsp | close |
| Smoked paprika | Sweet paprika + a drop of liquid smoke or chipotle | 1:1 | less smoke |
| Saffron | Turmeric (colour only), a pinch | - | no saffron flavour |
| Vanilla extract | Vanilla paste, 1 tsp; seeds of ¼ pod | per tsp | paste stronger |

## Vegetables, nuts and others

| Missing | Use | Ratio | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pine nuts (pesto) | Cashews, sunflower seeds, walnuts | 1:1 | cashews creamier; walnuts bitter |
| Almonds (ground) | Ground hazelnuts, sunflower seeds, oats (blitzed) | 1:1 | oats drier: add 1 tbsp oil per 100 g |
| Spinach | Chard, kale (cook longer), watercress, rocket (wilts faster) | 1:1 | kale tougher |
| Courgette | Aubergine, yellow squash, cucumber (raw only) | 1:1 | aubergine absorbs more oil |
| Mushrooms | Aubergine (umami), tinned jackfruit, cooked lentils | 1:1 | texture differs |
| Tinned tomatoes | 500 g fresh ripe tomatoes, cooked down; 200 ml passata + 2 tbsp purée | per 400 g tin | fresh need longer |
| Pasta | Rice, noodles, gnocchi, pearl barley, couscous | 1:1 dry weight | cooking time and sauce absorption change |
| Rice | Quinoa, bulgur, couscous, orzo, pearl barley | 1:1 dry | timing: couscous 5 min, barley 40 |
| Tofu | Tempeh, paneer, halloumi, chickpeas | 1:1 | all firmer; chickpeas do not brown |
| Chicken breast | Thighs (juicier), pork loin, firm fish, halloumi | 1:1 | thighs need 5–10 min longer |
| Minced beef | Minced pork, turkey, lamb; lentils (cooked, 1:1) | 1:1 | turkey drier: add fat |
| Chocolate (dark) | Cocoa 3 tbsp + 1 tbsp butter per 30 g | per 30 g | less snap, fine in cakes |
