# Classic ratios

Ratios are by **weight** unless marked. Each is a starting point; the notes say what to change and why.

## Doughs

| Dough | Ratio (flour : water : other) | Baker's % | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean bread (sandwich, rolls) | 5 : 3 | water 60–65, salt 2, instant yeast 1 | 1 % yeast at room temperature rises in 1–2 h; use 0.3 % for an overnight fridge rise |
| Baguette, ciabatta | - | water 70–80, salt 2, yeast 0.5–1 | wet dough, stretch-and-fold not kneading; hot oven with steam |
| Pizza (Neapolitan style) | - | water 60–65, salt 2.5–3, yeast 0.2–0.5 | long cold ferment 24–72 h; 250–280 g balls for 30 cm |
| Sourdough | - | water 70–78, salt 2, starter 20 (100 % hydration) | starter counts as half flour, half water; bulk until 50–75 % risen |
| Focaccia | - | water 75–85, salt 2, yeast 1, olive oil 5–8 | dimple with oiled fingers; brine on top |
| Brioche, enriched | - | milk 20–30, egg 25–50, butter 25–50, sugar 10–15, salt 2, yeast 1.5 | add butter last, cold ferment to shape |
| Pasta | 3 : 2 (flour : egg) | - | 100 g 00 flour to 1 egg (about 55 g); rest 30 min |
| Flatbread (no yeast) | 2 : 1 (flour : water) | salt 1.5, oil 5 | rest 20 min; hot dry pan |
| Pie / shortcrust pastry | 3 : 2 : 1 (flour : fat : water) | - | cold fat, minimal water; rest before rolling; blind bake |
| Rich shortcrust (pâte sucrée) | 2 : 1 : ½ : egg (flour : butter : sugar) + 1 egg per 250 g flour | - | fragile; chill well |
| Biscuit / scone (UK scone, US biscuit) | 3 : 1 : 2 (flour : fat : liquid) | baking powder 5 | cold fat, barely mix, tall cut |
| Rough puff | 1 : 1 (flour : butter) with water about 50 % of flour | - | 4 book folds, chill between |
| Choux | 2 : 1 : 1 : 2 (water : butter : flour : eggs) | - | eggs added until the paste falls in a V |

## Batters and cakes

| Item | Ratio | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pound cake / Victoria sponge | 1 : 1 : 1 : 1 (egg : flour : butter : sugar) | weigh the eggs in shell and match the rest; 1 tsp baking powder per 100 g flour if using plain flour |
| Génoise sponge | 1 : 1 : 1 (egg : sugar : flour), no fat or a little melted butter | whisk eggs and sugar to the ribbon; fold |
| Muffin | 2 : 2 : 1 : 1 (flour : liquid : egg : butter) + sugar to taste | mix wet into dry only until combined |
| Pancake (American) | 2 : 2 : 1 : ½ (flour : liquid : egg : butter) + 1 tsp baking powder per 100 g flour | rest 10 min |
| Crêpe | 1 : 1 : ½ (liquid : egg : flour) | thin; rest 30 min |
| Yorkshire pudding / popover | 2 : 1 : 1 (liquid : egg : flour), by volume works too | very hot fat, do not open the oven |
| Tempura / beer batter | 1 : 1 (flour : ice-cold liquid), by volume | barely mixed, lumps fine |
| Cookie (basic) | 1 : 2 : 3 (sugar : fat : flour) | more sugar spreads, more flour cakes |
| Brownie | 1 : 1 : 1 : ½ (sugar : butter+chocolate : egg : flour) approximately | underbake |
| Crumble topping | 2 : 1 : 1 (flour : butter : sugar) | add oats or nuts up to half the flour |

## Custards, creams and sauces

| Item | Ratio | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Custard (baked, crème caramel) | 2 : 1 (dairy : egg) | bain-marie, 82 °C |
| Crème anglaise | 4 : 1 : 1 (dairy : yolk : sugar) | to 82–84 °C, strain |
| Pastry cream | per 500 ml milk: 4 yolks, 100 g sugar, 40 g cornflour | boil 1 min while whisking |
| Caramel sauce | 1 : 1 (sugar : cream) + butter 25 % | add cream off the heat, carefully |
| Ganache | 1 : 1 dark (chocolate : cream) for truffles; 2 : 1 for firm; 1 : 2 for pouring | milk chocolate 2 : 1, white 3 : 1 |
| Vinaigrette | 3 : 1 (oil : acid) | 2 : 1 for sharper; add mustard to emulsify |
| Mayonnaise | 1 yolk : 150–200 ml oil | slow at first |
| Roux | 1 : 1 (butter : flour); 60 g roux thickens 1 litre to a sauce | cook out 2 min |
| Beurre blanc | 1 : 4 (reduction : cold butter) | never boil |
| Brine | 20 : 1 (water : salt) = 5 %; 15 : 1 for quick brines | poultry 4–12 h, pork chops 1–2 h |
| Stock | 3 : 2 (water : bones) | bare simmer |
| Simple syrup | 1 : 1 (sugar : water); rich 2 : 1 | |
| Rice (absorption) | 1 : 1.5 (rice : water) long grain; 1 : 1.25 soaked basmati; 1 : 1.1 short-grain sushi rice | lid on, off the heat 10 min |
| Risotto | 1 : 3 to 3.5 (rice : hot stock) | 17–18 min |
| Polenta | 1 : 4 (polenta : liquid) soft; 1 : 3 firm | |
| Porridge | 1 : 3 (oats : liquid) by volume | |
