# Portions for a crowd

Raw weights per person for a seated meal with a main, two sides and a dessert. Round totals up to what the shop sells.

## Mains

| Dish type | Per person | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boneless meat (fillet, loin, boneless joint, chicken breast or thigh fillet) | 150–200 g | 180 g is the safe middle; 200 g for a single main with few sides |
| Bone-in meat (chops, bone-in thighs, shoulder on the bone, ribs) | 250–350 g | bones are a third or more of the weight |
| Whole bird (chicken, turkey, duck) on the bone | 400–500 g | a 1.8 kg chicken feeds 4; a 5 kg turkey feeds 10–12 with leftovers |
| Fish fillet | 150–180 g | 200 g for whole fish on the bone |
| Prawns (shell on) | 200–250 g | 120 g peeled |
| Vegetarian or vegan main (tofu, tempeh, paneer, pulses cooked) | 150–200 g | 80 g dried pulses per head |
| Soup as a starter | 250 ml | 400 ml as a light main with bread |

## Canapés and buffets

| Situation | Per person |
| --- | --- |
| Canapés before dinner | 4–6 per hour |
| Canapés as the meal (drinks party, 3 hours) | 10–12 total, at least a third substantial |
| Buffet main items | 180–200 g protein total across dishes; 150 g starch; 150 g vegetables and salads; 120 g dessert |
| Finger food for children | 6–8 items, half of them plain (sandwiches, crudités, fruit) |
| Sandwiches as the meal | 1½ rounds (3 slices of bread) per adult |

## Drinks and ice

| Item | Per person |
| --- | --- |
| Wine | half a bottle per adult per two hours; 6 glasses per 750 ml bottle at 125 ml |
| Sparkling wine for a toast | 1 glass per adult; 7 flutes per bottle |
| Beer | 2 bottles or cans per adult per two hours at a barbecue |
| Soft drinks and water | 500 ml per person; double in hot weather or for a barbecue |
| Alcohol-free option | at least one planned drink, not just water |
| Ice | 500 g per person; 1 kg if drinks are served over ice all evening |
| Tea and coffee after dinner | 1 cup per person; 7 g coffee per cup |

## Adjustments

- **Buffet**: reduce each dish by 10 percent at 12–20 guests and by 20 percent above 20, because people take a little of everything. Do not reduce below two thirds of the seated portion.
- **Many dishes**: with four or more mains or sides, scale each to a share of the per-head total rather than full portions of each.
- **Teenagers**: add 25 percent to mains, starch and bread.
- **Children (under 12)**: half an adult portion; under fives a third.
- **Standing versus seated**: standing guests eat about 10 percent less and drink about 10 percent more.
- **Long events**: add 25 percent to drinks and 15 percent to food for every two hours beyond the first three.
- **Leftovers wanted**: add 20 percent and plan what the leftovers become.
