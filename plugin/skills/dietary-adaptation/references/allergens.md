# Allergens: regulated lists and hidden sources

## Regulated allergens

UK and EU (14): celery (including celeriac), cereals containing gluten (wheat, rye, barley, oats, spelt, kamut), crustaceans, eggs, fish, lupin, milk, molluscs, mustard, peanuts, sesame, soya, sulphur dioxide and sulphites above 10 mg/kg, tree nuts (almond, hazelnut, walnut, cashew, pecan, Brazil, pistachio, macadamia).

United States (9): milk, eggs, fish, crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soybeans, sesame.

Canada adds mustard and sulphites; Australia and New Zealand add lupin and sulphites; Japan lists shrimp, crab, wheat, buckwheat, egg, milk, peanut and walnut as mandatory.

## Hidden sources to check

| Allergen | Often hidden in |
| --- | --- |
| Gluten (wheat, barley, rye) | soy sauce, stock cubes and powders, gravy granules, malt vinegar, beer, barley in soups, couscous, bulgur, seitan, many sausages and burgers (rusk), breaded or battered items, some spice blends and baking powder, oats unless labelled gluten-free, blue cheese (traditional), communion wafers |
| Milk | butter, ghee (trace), whey in bread and crisps, "non-dairy" creamers with caseinate, milk chocolate, some margarines, lactose in medicines, pesto, many sauces and curries |
| Egg | mayonnaise, aioli, hollandaise, fresh pasta, meringue, royal icing, glazes on pastry, some noodles, lecithin (E322) can be egg or soya, foam on cocktails, binders in burgers and meatballs |
| Peanut | satay, some chilli sauces, groundnut oil, mixed nuts, many Asian and West African dishes, "arachis" oil, some vegetarian mince |
| Tree nuts | pesto, marzipan and frangipane, praline, nut oils and butters, some pestos use cashew, amaretto and frangelico, mortadella (pistachio), romesco (almond), Waldorf salad |
| Fish | Worcestershire sauce, Caesar dressing (anchovy), fish sauce (nam pla), some kimchi, dashi, oyster sauce (molluscs), many Thai and Vietnamese pastes, surimi |
| Crustaceans and molluscs | shrimp paste in curry pastes and laksa, oyster sauce, XO sauce, some fish stocks, paella stock |
| Soya | soy sauce, tofu, tempeh, miso, edamame, textured vegetable protein, lecithin (E322), many breads and chocolates, some vegetable oils (refined soya oil is generally tolerated but check) |
| Sesame | tahini, hummus, halva, sesame oil, many bread toppings, za'atar, dukkah, some falafel mixes, gomashio |
| Mustard | many salad dressings, mayonnaise, pickles, chutneys, curry powders, sausages, Dijon in sauces |
| Celery | stock cubes, soups, mixed spice blends, celery salt in Bloody Mary, some sausages |
| Sulphites | wine, cider, dried fruit (apricots), some vinegars, processed potatoes, prawns, some sausages |
| Lupin | some gluten-free flours and baked goods, especially from continental Europe |

## Cross-contact in a home kitchen

- Shared toaster, chopping boards, wooden spoons, colanders and fryer oil carry gluten, nuts and shellfish between dishes.
- Flour dust stays airborne for a while; make the allergen-free dish first, on a cleaned surface.
- Nut butters and tahini cling to jars and knives; use a clean knife every time.
- Check "may contain" statements; they matter for a diagnosed allergy and are optional for a preference.

## Swap behaviour that changes the method

- Gluten-free flour blends: add 10 to 20 percent more liquid, rest batters 15 minutes, use xanthan gum (¼ tsp per 100 g) for structure unless the blend has it.
- Egg replacers: 1 egg ≈ 3 tbsp aquafaba (whips), 1 tbsp ground flax + 3 tbsp water (binds, 10 min rest), 60 g mashed banana or apple purée (binds and sweetens), commercial replacer per packet. None sets a custard; use cornflour and a little more fat instead.
- Dairy: oat and soya milks behave closest to cow's milk in sauces; coconut cream for richness; nutritional yeast for a cheese note; vegan butter for pastry, chilled hard.
- Soy sauce → tamari (check gluten-free label) or coconut aminos (sweeter, use more).
- Fish sauce → light soy or tamari with a squeeze of lime and a pinch of sugar; or a vegan fish sauce.
- Nuts in pesto → sunflower or pumpkin seeds; in crumbles → oats and seeds.
